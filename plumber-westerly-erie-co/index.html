What Westerly Is

Westerly is a master-planned community in Erie developed by Southern Land Company. The company broke ground in 2021 at Erie Parkway and County Road 5, and described the community at that stage as about 400 acres and approximately 1,200 homes. A 2024 ColoradoBiz article says the community has since grown to 800 acres and is expected to have more than 3,000 homes when complete.

Home sales began in March 2023 and the first move-ins followed in April 2023. The builders named in that article are Brightland Homes, McStain Neighborhoods and SLC Homes. Construction continues, so neighbors in Westerly may have homes finished a year or more apart.

Amenities And Open Space

ColoradoBiz reports that The Waypoint amenity center opened in 2024, with a fitness studio, a community room and a resort-style pool. A second, larger amenity center is planned north of Erie Parkway. Nearly half of the community is set aside for pocket parks, open space and trails.

A New Home Starts With Your Builder's Warranty

Homes this new are within the age where builders and their warranties are the first call. Warranties differ in what they cover and for how long, and some require you to report problems through the builder. Read yours before you pay for a repair.

Not every problem in a new home is a defect, and not every defect is covered. A plumber can find the actual cause, which helps you decide whether it is a warranty matter.

How To Document A Plumbing Problem

Steps that help with a warranty claim or any repair

  • Take clear photos or short videos of the problem, including any water damage.
  • Note the date it started and anything that changed just before.
  • Shut off the water at the fixture's valve if it is leaking, and note where the valve is.
  • Keep your closing papers and warranty contact details handy.
  • Ask the plumber to write down what they found, even if the builder fixes it.

Who Owns Which Pipe

The Town of Erie owns the water line through the meter pit and five feet past the meter. The line after that is the property owner's. Utility Billing at 303-926-2700 can confirm for your address.
