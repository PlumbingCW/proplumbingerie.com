About Colliers Hill

Colliers Hill is served by Colliers Hill Metropolitan Districts Nos. 2 and 3. Their annual report places the districts in Weld County, within the Town of Erie, and notes that residential construction is ongoing by various builders. District No. 2 was organized on June 19, 2008.

The report lists district-financed improvements on Colliers Boulevard, Horizon Boulevard and Pinecliff Drive, along with parks, a spine trail and landscaping with irrigation. It also says there is no commercial land use within the districts, so this is a residential area. Builder listings mention a clubhouse, community center, pool and trails.

Where The Name Comes From

Local reporting says Colliers Boulevard honors the mining industry, because colliers were miners. Erie's coal-mining history runs through the town, and its last mine closed in 1978. We cannot say from our sources how the neighborhood itself was named, only that the street name points to that history.

Town Pipes And Your Pipes

According to the district's report, its water, storm sewer and sanitary sewer improvements were dedicated to the Town of Erie. The Town also owns the water line through the meter pit and five feet past the meter. The line from there to your house is the owner's, and Utility Billing at 303-926-2700 can confirm where it starts for your address.

Outdoor Water And Freeze Protection

This section is general plumbing information. Erie sees real freezes, and outdoor faucets are the usual place a pipe splits. Disconnect hoses before the first hard freeze, shut off and drain any interior valve that feeds the faucet, and leave the outdoor handle open so any water left can expand.

A split pipe often shows up only after the thaw, when water starts moving again. If a faucet drips steadily or the wall behind it is damp in spring, shut off the water to that line and call. See burst pipe repair for what to do in the first minutes.

Outdoor faucets and hose bibs

Drips, leaks at the handle or a faucet that was frozen over winter. See faucet repair.

Hidden water use

A meter that moves with every tap closed, or a wet patch in the yard. See leak detection.

Water heaters

Water pressure or line problems
