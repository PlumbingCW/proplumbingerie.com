What To Tell The Dispatcher

Have these ready when you call

  • The street address, including the town or ZIP code
  • What you see, hear or smell, in plain words
  • Whether you have already shut off the water, and where the main valve is
  • A phone number we can reach if the call drops
  • Whether this is a home or a business

Emergency Or Scheduled Visit

Burst pipes, sewage backups and water heater leaks go to the emergency line any hour. A slow drain, a dripping faucet or a planned installation can usually be booked as a same-day or next-day appointment when the schedule allows.

Not sure which one you have? Call and describe it. The dispatcher will say whether it can wait. You can also browse the services page or read about our service area.
