Basement Waterproofing in Erie, CO

Basement water comes from three places. Surface water runs toward the foundation, groundwater rises under the slab and plumbing leaks inside the house. Each calls for a different fix, and treating the wrong one wastes money.

We look at the source first. If the problem involves drainage or pumps, we can handle it. If it needs a foundation contractor or a grading crew, we tell you. Pumps and discharge lines fall under sump pump installation and repair.

A simple test tells you a lot. Tape a small square of plastic to the wall for a day. Moisture on the room side points to condensation. Moisture behind the plastic points to water coming through the wall.

Colorado Soil And Foundation Water

Many Front Range homes sit on clay that swells when wet. The Colorado Geological Survey's swelling soils guide says keeping water away from foundations is a main defense, which means grading that slopes away, downspouts that discharge several feet out and no heavy watering at the wall.

Erie averages roughly 16 inches of precipitation a year, so the problem is often timing, not volume. Spring snowmelt and short intense storms deliver a lot of water at once.

We do not do structural foundation repair or exterior excavation of whole walls. When that is the real need, we say so and suggest you ask a foundation specialist.

According to the Colorado Geological Survey, clay soils that swell when wet can lift slabs and stress buried pipes, so drainage work has to keep water moving away from the structure. Plumbing codes such as the International Plumbing Code (IPC) govern sump pits and discharge piping, and the plumber confirms local rules with the building office.

Gutters, Downspouts And Window Wells

Gutters that overflow dump roof water next to the wall, and downspouts that end at the foundation do the same. Extend each downspout several feet from the house and keep window wells clear of leaves and debris. These are inexpensive steps that often reduce basement moisture before any digging is needed.

Protecting What Is Below Grade

Keep boxes off the floor, store valuables on shelves and avoid carpet on the slab in a damp room. A dehumidifier helps with humidity, but it does not fix leaks. Mold needs moisture, so the water source always has to be addressed.
