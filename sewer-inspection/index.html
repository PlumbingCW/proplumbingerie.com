Sewer Inspection in Erie, CO

A sewer camera inspection shows the inside of the line without digging. A small camera on a flexible rod travels through the cleanout and records the pipe condition, the materials and the location of any damage. It turns a guess into a picture.

Use it before buying a house, before a remodel, after a backup or when a clog keeps returning.

What The Camera Shows

The camera reveals cracks, offset joints, root intrusion, bellies where water pools, scale buildup and foreign objects. It also identifies the pipe material and shows where the line connects to the city main, so the plumber can tell you whether a problem is yours or the utility's.

Cracks and breaks

Hairline cracks and broken sections show as lines or gaps in the pipe wall.

Roots

Roots enter at joints and spread into the line, often visible as a thick mat.

Bellies

A sag holds water and waste, which causes repeat clogs.

Scale and grease

Buildup narrows the pipe and signals the need for jetting.

When An Inspection Pays Off

Buyers use inspections to avoid inheriting a failing line. Homeowners use them before landscaping, a bathroom addition or a driveway project. Anyone with repeat backups should look before paying for another cleaning.

Erie Homes And What To Expect

Erie had 6,291 residents in 2000 and 30,038 in 2020, so many neighborhoods have newer plastic lines in good condition. Inspections of those homes often confirm a sound line, which is useful information in itself. Older Old Town lines are more likely to show cast iron scale or clay joints.

Ground movement also matters. The Colorado Geological Survey describes swelling soils that can shift buried lines, and a camera is the easiest way to see whether that has happened.

What A Good Inspection Report Includes

A useful report is more than a video file. It names the pipe material, states the length inspected, marks the distance to each defect and says whether the defect is minor, moderate or serious. A plain-language summary lets a buyer, a seller or an insurer understand it without watching the whole recording.

Ask for these items in the report

  • Pipe material and approximate diameter at the access point
  • Distance from the cleanout to each crack, offset, belly or root mass
  • Whether the line connects cleanly to the city main
  • Photos or video clips of every defect noted
  • A recommendation that separates urgent repairs from watch items

Same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows, which helps when a closing date is close. Tell us the date and we will say what is possible.

Cleanouts are what make an inspection practical. Plumbing codes such as the International Plumbing Code (IPC) call for cleanouts at points such as the junction of the building drain and the building sewer. If your home has none, a toilet pull is the fallback and a new cleanout is a worthwhile addition.
