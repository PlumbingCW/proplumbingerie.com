Emergency And Urgent Repairs

When water is moving where it should not, start with burst pipe repair for a pipe that has split, or leak detection when you can hear or see signs of water but not the source. A line leaking in the yard is covered by water service line repair.

Sewage in the house is a sewer line cleaning call when the line is blocked and a sewer line repair call when it is broken. A sewer camera inspection shows which one it is.

Drains And Sewer Lines

A clogged sink, tub or toilet starts with drain cleaning. When grease or scale keeps bringing the clog back, hydro jetting cleans the pipe wall instead of poking through the buildup.

Kitchens that send waste through a disposal are covered under garbage disposal repair and installation. Shops and restaurants with floor drains, grease traps and heavy daily use can see commercial plumbing.

If you are unsure which of these fits, call (303) 552-3896 and describe the symptom. The dispatcher can tell you whether it needs a plumber tonight or can wait for a scheduled visit.

Water Heaters

A leaking tank or a unit with no hot water starts with water heater repair. When the tank has failed, water heater replacement covers sizing, venting and permits.

For a new location or a fuel change, see water heater installation. Households wanting continuous hot water can read about a tankless water heater, including descaling and gas line needs.

Fixtures And New Work

A running toilet or a wobbling base is covered by toilet repair and installation. Dripping spouts and failed supply lines are handled under faucet repair.

New bathrooms, kitchens and basement rough-ins fall under plumbing installation. Homes with failing supply pipes can look at whole house repiping.

Basements And Water Quality

Basement water has several sources. If a pump has failed or you need a backup, see sump pump installation and repair. For groundwater, grading and drainage, see basement waterproofing.

Scale on fixtures or a chlorine taste in the water points to water softener and filtration. We suggest a water test before you buy any equipment.
