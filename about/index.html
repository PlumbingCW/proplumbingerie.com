What You Can Expect From Us

We say what we will do and what it costs before we do it. If the job changes once a wall or pipe is opened, the plumber stops and explains the new price. We offer same-day appointments when the schedule allows, and we do not promise a fixed arrival time.

We are licensed and insured. Colorado licenses plumbers through the State Plumbing Board, and you can check any plumber on the board's licensee lookup.

What We Do And Do Not Do

We repair and install plumbing. That includes the services on our services page, from drain cleaning to water heater replacement and sewer line repair.

We do not do electrical work, furnace repair, appliance repair or structural foundation work. If a problem needs one of those, we say so and suggest you call the right trade.

Where We Work

Erie is home base. We also work in Lafayette, Louisville, Frederick, Firestone, Dacono and Broomfield. See the service area page and our Lafayette page for details.

How We Explain Pricing

Plumbing prices depend on what the plumber finds, so we do not publish a price list that would be wrong for half the jobs. Each service page lists the factors that move the price, such as the location of the problem, the tool or part needed, the access and the hour of the call.

Once the plumber has found the cause, you hear the price and the options before work starts. If the job changes after a wall or pipe is opened, the plumber stops and explains the new price before continuing. A price you did not agree to is not a price we expect you to pay.

Emergency Calls Versus Scheduled Work

Some problems cannot wait. A burst pipe, a sewage backup or a water heater leaking onto the floor goes to the emergency line, which is open 24/7. Others, such as a slow drain, a dripping faucet or a planned installation, can usually be booked as a same-day or next-day appointment when the schedule allows.

If you are not sure which one you have, call and describe it. The dispatcher will say whether it can wait, and what to shut off or avoid until a plumber arrives.

Helpful details to have ready are the fixture involved, when the problem started and whether water is still running. Photos of the damage are useful too. None of this is required to call, and the dispatcher will ask for what is needed.

Safety Comes Before Plumbing

Some situations are not plumbing problems first. If you smell natural gas, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 before you call a plumber. If water is near electrical outlets or the panel, stay clear and switch off power only if you can do so without standing in water.

Our service pages include short checklists for the first few minutes of common emergencies, such as a burst pipe or a blocked main sewer line. They are meant to limit damage until a plumber arrives, not to replace one.

Checking Any Plumber Before You Hire

A licensed plumber in Colorado works under the State Plumbing Board, part of the Division of Professions and Occupations. Ask for the name of the licensed plumber who will do the work and the registered plumbing contractor behind them, and look both up before you agree to a job.

Ask who pulls the permit, who schedules the inspection and what the written price includes. A plumber who answers those questions plainly is easier to work with, whether you hire us or someone else.
