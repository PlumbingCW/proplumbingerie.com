Erie Across Two Counties

The 2020 Census counted 30,038 people in Erie, with about 58 percent living in Weld County and 42 percent in Boulder County. Permits, inspections and utility questions can differ by address, so tell the dispatcher your street and we sort out which office to contact.

Town of Erie Utility Billing is at 303-926-2700, and the Building Division, which handles permits and inspections, is at 303-926-2780. Both are at 645 Holbrook Street.

How We Handle Calls From Outside Erie

Calls from Lafayette, Louisville, Frederick, Firestone, Dacono and Broomfield go to the same 24/7 line as Erie calls. The dispatcher asks for the street address, the nearest cross street and what you can see, then explains what to shut off while a plumber is lined up. Arrival depends on the hour and call volume, so we do not promise a fixed window.

Each town has its own utility, building office and rules. The plumber starts by confirming who handles water service and permits at your address. For Erie addresses that is the Town of Erie, and for other towns we ask the local office before work that needs a permit.

Seasonal Calls Across The Area

Winter calls are mostly frozen and split pipes, outdoor spigots that failed over the cold months and water heaters working hard through long cold snaps. If a faucet gives only a trickle on a freezing morning, shut off the water to that line and call before the pipe splits.

Spring and storm calls shift to basements. Snowmelt and heavy rain test sump pumps, discharge lines and floor drains, and a pump without a battery backup stops when the power does. Our pages on sump pump repair and burst pipe repair cover the first steps for each.
