When A Clogged Drain Becomes An Emergency

A single slow sink can wait until morning. A backup cannot. When a toilet, a tub and a floor drain all misbehave together, the blockage is usually in the main line, and the next flush or load of laundry sends wastewater into the lowest fixture in the house.

That is why we ask which fixtures are affected before recommending anything. One clogged fixture points to a branch line. Several affected fixtures, gurgling sounds or water rising in a basement floor drain point to the main sewer line.

Signs the main line is blocked

  • Water backs up in a tub or shower when the toilet is flushed
  • A basement floor drain gurgles or shows standing water after laundry
  • More than one fixture drains slowly at the same time
  • A plunger clears the toilet briefly but the problem returns within a day
  • Sewage smell near a floor drain or cleanout

Snake, Jet Or Camera: Choosing The Method

The right tool depends on what is in the pipe, not on what costs more. A cable is quick and cheap for hair and paper. Grease and scale need cutting or water pressure, and repeat clogs need a look inside.

Drain Problems We See Around Erie

Erie grew from 6,291 residents in 2000 to 30,038 in 2020, so most homes here have plastic drain lines from the last few decades. Those lines rarely fail on their own. Wipes labeled flushable, kitchen grease and a bathroom full of hair cause most of the clogs we are called to clear.

Older homes in Old Town Erie are a different case. Clay or cast iron lines can sag, scale over or let roots in at the joints. If a drain in an older house keeps clogging, a camera look is worth more than another round of snaking.

Slope matters as much as the clog. The International Plumbing Code (IPC) calls for horizontal drain lines of two and a half inches or smaller to fall at least one quarter inch per foot. A run that sags below that slope holds water and waste, which is why a belly in the line keeps bringing clogs back after every cleaning.

Keeping Drains Clear Afterward

Grease goes in the trash, not the sink, and a hair catch in the tub costs a few dollars. Only toilet paper and waste belong in a toilet, whatever the packaging says. Running hot water for a minute after washing dishes moves soft residue past the trap.

If a kitchen line has clogged more than once, ask for hydro jetting, which scours the pipe wall instead of poking a hole through the buildup. For a clog that keeps coming back from the main, sewer line cleaning and a camera inspection find the cause.

Where Drain Cleaning Fits With Other Work

A clog is sometimes the first sign of a bigger repair. When the camera shows a cracked or bellied pipe, the next step is sewer line repair. When the clog is in a new bathroom or kitchen line, the issue may be slope or venting, which falls under plumbing installation.
