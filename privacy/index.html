Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Erie Pro Plumbing collects, how we use it and what choices you have. It applies to this website and to calls to our phone line.

Who We Are

Erie Pro Plumbing is a plumbing business at 149 S Briggs St, Erie, CO 80516. You can reach us at (303) 552-3896 or contact@proplumbingerie.com.

Information From Phone Calls

When you call, we may collect your name, phone number, address and a description of your plumbing problem. We use this information to dispatch a plumber, give you a price and follow up on the work.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Information From This Website

This website does not have a contact form and does not ask you to create an account. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, which can include your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit.

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and some pages show a Google Maps view. When those load, Google may receive your IP address and device details under its own privacy policy.

Email

If you email us, we keep your message and address so we can reply. We do not use your email for unrelated marketing.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with people who help us do the job, such as a licensed plumber assigned to your visit, and when the law requires it.

How Long We Keep It

We keep job and call records for as long as needed to support the work, answer questions and meet legal and tax obligations. After that we delete or anonymize them.

Your Choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it, subject to legal limits. Email contact@proplumbingerie.com or call (303) 552-3896.

Children

This site is meant for adults who own or manage property. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy. The current version is the one on this page.

Coverage Area

Where We Dispatch Around Erie

Our coverage centers on Erie in Weld County and reaches the nearby towns of Lafayette, Louisville, Frederick, Firestone, Dacono and Broomfield. Call the 24/7 line and we confirm your address before dispatch.
