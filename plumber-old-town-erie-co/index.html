Where Old Town Sits In Erie

Old Town is the historic center of the Town of Erie, in Weld County, ZIP 80516. Briggs Street runs through it and is the town's main shopping and dining corridor, according to the Homes.com local guide to the neighborhood. Festival Plaza at Public Road and Chester Street and the Art Center of Erie at 625 Pierce Street are two landmarks people use to describe where they are.

None of the sources we checked publish a formal boundary for Old Town, so we describe it by anchor streets rather than draw a line. If your address is near Briggs or Pierce Street and you are unsure whether it counts, say so on the phone and we will confirm the address before anything else.

What The Housing Looks Like

Homes.com lists about 304 homes in the neighborhood, with a median year built of 1951. Its guide describes a mix that runs from early 1900s miner cabins to 1970s ranch homes and remodeled multi-story and Victorian houses, along with some townhouses and condos. Those are listing-site figures, not a Town count, so treat them as a rough picture.

The town grew around coal mining, and the last mine closed in 1978, according to a 9News history of Erie. That history is why Old Town has older streets than most of Erie. It does not tell us what is inside the walls of any single house.

An Older House Is A Reason To Look, Not A Diagnosis

Age alone does not mean a home has failing pipes. Over many decades, most older houses have had parts of their plumbing replaced at different times, so one house can have several materials in it. The only way to know is to look.

A sewer camera inspection shows the condition of the drain line from inside the pipe. A plumber can also check the supply side and the water service line before recommending any repair. Colorado banned lead pipe in 1988, and the Town of Erie reports no town-owned lead lines, but the pipe on your side of the meter is yours to check.

Calls We Hear From Old Town Homes

Slow or backed-up drains

A kitchen sink or tub that drains slowly, or a toilet that gurgles, can come from a fixture trap, a branch line or the main. See drain cleaning.

Water heater trouble

Lukewarm water, a leaking tank or a pilot that will not stay lit. See water heater repair and replacement.

Leaks and high water bills

A wet spot you cannot trace or a meter that keeps moving. See leak detection.

Water line between meter and house

The line from the meter pit to the house is the owner's side. See water service line repair.

Who Owns Which Pipe

The Town of Erie owns the water line through the meter pit and five feet past the meter. Everything after that, running to your house, is the property owner's responsibility. Utility Billing at 303-926-2700 can confirm where the Town's line ends for your address.

Plumbing For Businesses Near Briggs Street

Old Town mixes homes with restaurants, shops and offices. Commercial kitchens and public restrooms put more load on floor drains and sewer lines than a house does, and a blockage can close a business for the day. Our commercial plumbing page explains what we can help with.

Before You Call

Useful details to have ready

  • Which fixture is affected and when the problem started.
  • Whether the water is still running, and if so where the shutoff valve is.
  • Any photos of the damage or the fixture.
  • A rough idea of the age of the fixture or water heater, if you know it.

Permit questions go to the Town of Erie Building Division at 303-926-2780, and permits are handled through the Town's online portal. Which jobs need one depends on the work.
