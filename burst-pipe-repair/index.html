Burst Pipe Emergency: The First Ten Minutes

1

Shut off the main

Turn the main valve clockwise until it stops. If you cannot find it, close the meter-side valve.

2

Cut power if needed

Switch off breakers for wet areas. Do not step into standing water near outlets.

3

Drain the lines

Open a faucet at the lowest point and one at the highest to drain remaining water.

4

Photograph and call

Document the damage and call us and your insurer.

Do not use heat from an open flame to thaw pipe. A hair dryer or warm towels are safer if a pipe is frozen but not yet burst.

Why Pipes Burst

Freezing is the best-known cause. Water expands as it turns to ice and the pressure splits the pipe, often at a weak point or fitting. The break usually shows after thaw, when water flows again.

Other causes include corrosion, high water pressure, a failed fitting and ground movement. A pipe that burst without cold weather may signal a system problem, so the plumber checks pressure and the surrounding lines.

Frozen Pipes Before They Burst

Colorado winters can drop quickly. Open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls, let a faucet drip during a hard freeze and disconnect hoses. If a faucet gives only a trickle in freezing weather, a pipe may be frozen. Call before it splits.

Insulating pipes in unheated spaces, sealing air leaks near pipes and keeping the heat on in vacant rooms help prevent freezes.

Cleaning Up Safely After A Burst

Clean water from a burst supply pipe is a different problem from sewage. Remove standing water, pull up soaked rugs and run fans and a dehumidifier. Drywall and insulation that stayed wet more than a day or two usually need to come out.

Same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows, and a pipe that is still running goes to the 24/7 line at any hour. After the water is off and the pipe is repaired, a restoration company can handle drying and mold prevention.

Pressure can be part of the cause. Plumbing codes such as the International Plumbing Code (IPC) call for a pressure reducing valve when static pressure exceeds 80 psi, and a failed regulator can push supply pressure high enough to split a weak fitting. The plumber reads the pressure during the repair.
