What Erie Highlands Is

Erie Highlands is a residential development in the Town of Erie. Erie Highlands Metropolitan Districts Nos. 1 and 2 were formed in 2013 to serve it, and the districts state that they are located entirely within the Town of Erie in Weld County. Because the districts were formed in 2013, homes here are newer than the ones in Old Town.

Builder listings show new homes still being added. Trumark Homes lists 116 townhomes of about 1,548 to 1,682 square feet in the community, with a sales office at 260 North Vista Drive. Its page describes them as coming soon, so treat that as a sales listing, not a finished count.

What The Districts Handle

The district's governance page says the districts were formed to finance and operate water, parks and recreation, sanitation, streets, safety protection and storm drainage. They also handle covenant enforcement, design review and landscaping on district-owned tracts, and they operate the pool and clubhouse. Those are infrastructure and shared amenities. They are not a substitute for a plumber inside your home.

The Town owns the water line through the meter pit and five feet past the meter. After that the line to your house is the owner's. If you are unsure where your responsibility starts, Utility Billing can tell you for your address.

Townhomes Share Walls And Risks

Where homes are attached, a leak in one unit can reach the next. This is general plumbing information. If water is coming through a ceiling or wall from a neighboring unit, shut off the water to your own unit, tell your neighbor and the association, take photos, and then call a plumber.

Builder Warranty Comes First On A New Home

If your home is new, check your builder's warranty before you pay for a repair. Warranties differ in what they cover and how long, and some require you to use the builder's own contacts for certain problems. Read yours, and ask the builder if you are unsure.

A plumber can still help by finding the cause and documenting it with photos. That record is useful whether the fix is under warranty or not.
