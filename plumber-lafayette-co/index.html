Before We Arrive

These steps limit damage in most Lafayette emergencies. None needs tools, and each takes only a few minutes.

1

Shut off the water

Turn the main shutoff clockwise until it stops. It is usually near where the line enters the house, in a basement or utility closet.

2

Cut power near water

Switch off breakers for wet areas. Stay out of standing water if outlets or appliances are nearby.

3

Contain the spread

Move rugs and boxes, set towels or a bucket under drips and open cabinet doors under affected sinks.

4

Document everything

Photograph the source and the damage before you clean up. Insurers ask for photos and the plumber sees what changed.

Lafayette Water, Meters And Mains

Lafayette runs its own water system, separate from Erie's. According to the City, water is treated at the Baseline Water Treatment Plant, which handles 13.0 million gallons a day, using snowmelt from South Boulder Creek, Boulder Creek and Coal Creek stored in the Baseline and Waneka reservoirs. Colorado-Big Thompson and Windy Gap supplies are also available.

The City serves roughly 9,443 connections and is rolling out smart meters, and it began a leak detection survey of its system in 2019. If your meter keeps running with every tap closed, call us, and ask the City about its policies on meter ownership and leak adjustments.

Old Town And Newer Neighborhoods

Lafayette was founded in 1888 by Mary E. Miller after coal was found nearby in 1884, and the Simpson Mine opened in 1887. The last mine, Black Diamond, closed in 1956. Old Town, centered on Festival Plaza at Public Road and Chester Street, still holds some of the city's oldest buildings.

Waneka Lake Park, a 147-acre park at West Emma Street and Caria Drive, sits on a reservoir the city bought in October 1972. Homes and shops around these landmarks vary in age, so the plumber checks pipe material before recommending a repair.

Winter Freezes And Spring Thaw In Lafayette

Lafayette sits at about 5,266 feet, and cold snaps there can freeze pipes in unheated garages, exterior walls and crawlspaces. A split pipe often shows up only after the thaw, when water starts flowing again. If a faucet gives a trickle on a freezing morning, shut off the water to that line and call before it splits.

Spring brings a different problem. Snowmelt and storms can overwhelm a basement sump pump, and a pump with no battery backup stops when the power does. Our pages on burst pipe repair and sump pump repair explain what to do in each case.
